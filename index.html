📖 How to Hunt for Planet X

1. The Blink Comparator: Click "Auto-Blink" to flip between Month 0 and Month 6 passes. Stationary stars don't move, but a moving planet or brown dwarf jumps across the screen!

2. Difference Subtraction: Select "Difference (E2 - E1)" to cancel stationary stars. Moving targets leave adjacent cyan (Epoch 1 deficit) and magenta (Epoch 2 excess) dipoles.

3. 102-Band Spectrogram: Cold bodies peak at thermal infrared (4.5–5.0 µm) with deep methane absorption at 3.3 µm.
